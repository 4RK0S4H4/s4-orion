Getting Paid Faster

Three habits that cut the time between sending an invoice and seeing the money.

Updated Nov 12 · 4 min read

Send Invoices the Same Day

Clients tend to pay in the order invoices arrive. An invoice sent the day the work ends lands before the month’s batch of bills, not after it. See how we measured this.

Make Paying One Tap

Every invoice includes a Pay Now link. Turn on card and bank payments so clients never have to type an account number:

  1. Open Settings and choose Payments.
  2. Turn on Card and Bank Transfer.
  3. Send a test invoice to yourself.
Invoices with a payment link were paid 9 days sooner on average.

Remind Without Nagging

Automatic reminders go out three days before the due date and on the day it is due. You can change the wording in Settings › Reminders.

Still Stuck?

Write to us from the app and we’ll answer within a day.
