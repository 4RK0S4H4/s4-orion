S4 Orion: Direction Lab

The new direction, in your words: leave Material 3 Expressive behind and make Orion its own thing.

  • No sharp corners, anywhere.
  • Flat: content sits on the page as plain shapes and tones, no layers of cards.
  • Depth only through blur: floating chrome and overlays are frosted glass (today's glass, AA guaranteed). No shadows.
  • Simpler motion than the container transform: short fades and slides that look the same in every browser.
  • Works everywhere, phone and desktop, like a real framework.

What stays: monochrome by default with one optional seed color, Inter, Solar icons, Title Case labels, the glass contrast guarantee and its solid fallbacks (high contrast, reduced transparency, browsers without blur).

Compatibility target: Safari and iOS 15.4+, Chrome and Edge 100+, Firefox 103+. Plain CSS and a small script, no build step, usable from plain HTML, React, Vue, Svelte or server pages. Every effect has a fallback: no blur means solid, no corner-shape means plain round corners, reduced motion means fades only.
