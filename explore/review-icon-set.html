S4 Orion: Icon Set

You like Solar Bold Duotone and want alternatives in a similar style. Phosphor has no bold duotone, so there are three: Solar (today), Phosphor Duotone as it ships (lighter strokes with a tint), and Phosphor Bold Duotone, which I composed from Phosphor's bold outlines over its duotone tint to get closer to Solar's weight.

You can also switch live in the showcase app: Settings, Icon Style. Tap the card above its preview to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right.
