S4 Orion: Button Groups

A group of buttons where you pick a value (a view, a period, a contrast level). It should look like one group and stay rounded, and it should not look like tabs. Each card shows only the group: a single choice, a smaller size, and a multi-choice icon group. Tap them to try.

Tap the card above its preview to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right.
