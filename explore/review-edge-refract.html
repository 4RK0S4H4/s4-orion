S4 Orion: Glass Rim Line

The edge line itself should act like glass: clear, showing what is behind it, and brighter than the backdrop. Here the tint stops just short of the edge so the rim line is clear glass, with a white lift over it. True refraction (bending the backdrop) only works in Chromium, so these are the Safari-safe versions. Shown on the colorful backdrop and on the pale page; the dark one shows the same option in dark mode.

Tap the card above its preview to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right (for example "B but lighter").
