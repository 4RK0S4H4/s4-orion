S4 Orion: Clearer Glass Edges

Glass edges that let more of what is behind show through, like light passing through the rim. The surface tint fades out toward the edge, so the blurred backdrop shows more there. True refraction (bending the backdrop) only works in Chromium, so these are the Safari-safe versions. Shown on the colorful backdrop and on the pale page; the dark one shows the same option in dark mode.

Tap the card above its preview to pick one; Other plus a note if none is right (for example "B but lighter").
