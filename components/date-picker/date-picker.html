Components / Date Picker

A date field opens a glass calendar instead of the browser's own picker. The markup stays a native <input type="date"> in a text field, so forms, min and max keep working and the page still works without scripts. Under its field on a desktop, a centered panel on a phone. Arrow keys move by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month.

Single Date

One with a date already set, one empty. Tap the month to jump to another month or year.

Electric Co., invoice 1042.

Min / Max and Disabled Dates

min and max come from the native attributes; data-disabled-dates blocks single days (here Thanksgiving and Christmas). The keyboard stops at the limits too.

Any business day until the end of the year.
Can't be in the future.

Range

data-range-end points to a second date input: the first tap sets the start, the second the end. Nothing changes until both are chosen.

Bills due in this period are included.

In a Form

The native input carries the value, so the form submits yyyy-mm-dd like any date field, and Reset restores it.
