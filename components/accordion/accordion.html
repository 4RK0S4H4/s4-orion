Components / Accordion

Sections that open and close, built on native details.

Single open

Items share a name, so opening one closes the others.

What is autopay?
Pays the bill from your default account on its due date.
Can I change the account?
Yes. Choose another account in Settings before the due date.
What if a payment fails?
You get a notification and the bill moves to Overdue until it is paid.
